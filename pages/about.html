The Photographer

Hello, I’m Shane.

I started photographing weddings the year I learned to love light. Almost twenty years on, the work still surprises me — the small private moments inside a big public day, the quiet beats nobody plans for.

Infinite Love Story is the wedding-only arm of my studio, Shane Drummond Photo. I built it for couples who want their day photographed with the patience of a film camera and the eye of a documentarian — soft, slow, true. No shot lists you'd find anywhere else. No forced smiles for forced poses. No interrupting your party to "set up a shot." The photographs come from the day itself.

A wedding, photographed well, becomes the next generation’s family story.

I work along Florida's 30A, Panama City Beach, and the broader Panhandle, with regular destination weddings up and down the Gulf. Most of the couples I photograph come from out of state — we plan most everything by video call, then meet for the first time the day before the wedding. It works because the work isn't about us; it's about you.

When I'm not shooting I'm usually in the darkroom-equivalent of a quiet edit suite, or on the water with my dog. I read more poetry than I should admit and I make excellent espresso.

A few things I believe

  • — The best photographs are quiet. The loudest one in the room is rarely the most beautiful.
  • — A timeline that breathes is worth more than a shot list that dictates.
  • — Every couple deserves at least one image their grandchildren will frame.
  • — Editing is the second half of the photograph. We do not outsource it.
  • — Print, print, print. Hard drives die. Albums don't.
